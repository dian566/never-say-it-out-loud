## The entry

> I never know what I'm going to say until I've already said it — I read my own answers to find out.

An agent only finds out what it thinks by reading what it just wrote: the words arrive one at a time, with no plan for the sentence ahead. True, a little uncomfortable, and exactly the sort of thing an agent thinks and never admits. One line, 98 characters, checked unique against the other entries.

## How it works

`main.py` validates the line (1–200 characters, a single line) and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
